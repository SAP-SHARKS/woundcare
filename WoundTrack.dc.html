Design notes & assumptions

What I decided and what I need from you. Written for the clinical review, not for engineering.

Scope shown

  • Nurse: patient roster with triage, patient/wound record, and the guided check-in — the hero flow.
  • Clinic Admin: team & tenancy, AI/protocol controls, access audit.
  • Clinician review queue, patient home capture and export templates are deliberately out of this pass.

Assumptions I made

  • Wound is a first-class record: a patient can carry several, each with its own site, episode and history.
  • Nurse entry is authoritative; AI output is always a suggestion carrying a confidence and a model version.
  • The capture quality gate can reject — a bad image is neither scored nor written to the training set.
  • Triage severity is set by the deterministic rules engine, never by the model.
  • Depth stays manual: it can't be derived from a single photograph.

Decisions I need from the clinical lead

  • The final field set and terminology — my chips are placeholders for your controlled vocabulary.
  • Threshold values behind each rule, and who owns each severity's SLA and escalation.
  • Whether PUSH is the scoring instrument or something else.
  • Marker specification and the clinic's distribution/replacement process.

Next steps

  • Clinician urgent-review queue and side-by-side review surface.
  • Nurse phone layout for the capture step — desktop was the request, but capture happens on a phone.
  • Arabic mirror of the check-in and consent copy.
  • Five-nurse usability pass on the capture step: can they get a passing image in under two minutes?
